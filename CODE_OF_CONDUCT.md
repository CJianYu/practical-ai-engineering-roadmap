# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Welcome learners asking sincere questions.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest when recommending commercial tools.
- Protect private data and respect licenses.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, spam, undisclosed promotion, and publishing private or proprietary material are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method on their GitHub profile. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
